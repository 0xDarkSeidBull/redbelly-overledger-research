A few paragraphs

Could Redbelly connect to Quant's Overledger? We looked only at public documents. Redbelly and Quant have not announced any partnership or talks, and this research does not suggest any.

Overledger is Quant's platform for reaching many blockchains through one interface. Its self-serve route covers EVM chains, and Redbelly is EVM compatible, so a basic connection, reading Redbelly and sending transactions the sender has signed, looks possible. It is untested, and Overledger describes self-serve as for public permissionless networks, while writing to Redbelly needs a credential. Every Redbelly rule would still apply.

Overledger's Fusion Rollup, its shared layer for moving tokens between chains, does not list Redbelly among its connected networks, and the docs describe no self-serve way to add a chain. As far as public sources show, that part is not possible today.

Receptor credentials and Tokeniser's eligibility rules are enforced on Redbelly. They do not travel with a token to another chain. Keeping regulated assets on Redbelly avoids that gap.

Open questions: whether Overledger would treat Redbelly as self-serve, whether Redbelly passes its network check, which chains The Clearing House network will reach, and Redbelly's own position on bridging. The full report and every source are linked below.
