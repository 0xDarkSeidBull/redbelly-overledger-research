1. Pre-Flight Warning.
Match the network on the exchange deposit page before you send.

Before you deposit RBNT to any centralized exchange, open its RBNT deposit page, read
the network it credits, and send on exactly that network. Exchanges listing RBNT credit it
on the redbelly network as native RBNT, not as an Ethereum erc-20 token. Sending the
wrong form to the wrong network is how deposits are lost.

The correct deposit procedure:

   1. Open the exchange, go to the RBNT deposit page, and read the network or chain
      selector
   2. If it says Redbelly Network, send native RBNT on the Redbelly Network to the
       address shown.

   3. If it says Ethereum (ERC-20), do not send native RBNT. Bridge native RBNT to
       wRBNT on Ethereum using Reddex or Lucid Labs first, then deposit the ERC-20
       wRBNT over Ethereum.

   4. Send 1 RBNT as a test amount first, confirm the exchange credits the deposit, then
       send the remaining balance.

3. Failure Mode 1: Wrapped RBNT Shows Zero Value or Fails to Swap

Bridge the tokens back to Redbelly Network or swap in the official Ethereum pool. A zero
dollar value means your wallet lacks a price feed, not that the balance is empty. Swaps
fail because you are trying them on a venue that has no wRBNT liquidity.

To swap wRBNT on Ethereum:

   1. Open app.uniswap.org in your browser.

   2. Click the top token dropdown, paste
      0xb45fFB51984d626Ee758b336C61Cf20990c6bF13, and select wRBNT.
   3. Click the bottom token dropdown and select ETH.




   4. Enter 1 or more in the wRBNT amount field.

   5. Click Swap.

   6. Click Confirm Swap and approve the transaction in your wallet.

Success Condition: The transaction succeeds, and your Ethereum wallet balance
reflects the received ETH.

4. Failure Mode 2: Stablecoins Stranded on Ethereum.

Use Reddex or Lucid Labs to move the stablecoins to Redbelly Network. A failed or
abandoned bridge attempt leaves the funds on the source chain.
To bridge stranded stablecoins to Redbelly:

   1. Open reddex.io in your browser.

   2. Click Connect Wallet and approve the connection.

   3. Click the Source Network dropdown and select Ethereum.




   4. Click the Destination Network dropdown and select Redbelly Network.




   5. Click the Token dropdown and select USDC or USDT.
   6. Enter the amount to bridge.

   7. Click Approve and sign the request in your wallet.

   8. Click Bridge and confirm the transaction in your wallet.

Success Condition: The stablecoins appear in your wallet on the Redbelly Network,
verifiable on redbelly.routescan.io.

5. Failure Mode 3: Stuck Bridge Transfers and Quote Errors.

Manually execute the transfer or retry the quote. Transfers bridged to Ethereum via
Polymer do not automatically relay. Many stuck transfers to Ethereum are just waiting on
this step.

To resolve stuck Ethereum transfers:

   1. Open app.lucidlabs.fi and connect the exact wallet you bridged from.

   2. Open Bridge History and find your transfer.

   3. Click Claim inside the Action column next to the transfer.

   4. Confirm the transaction in your wallet and pay the Ethereum gas fee.

Success Condition: The Bridge History status updates to Complete, and the
destination explorer shows the expected RBNT or wRBNT balance.

To resend a failed transfer or fix a quote error:
   1. Refresh the app.lucidlabs.fi page and enter 1 RBNT to test if the quote failure is
        due to route limits.

   2. If a transfer failed completely, open Bridge History and select the affected transfer.

   3. Click Resend Transaction.

   4. Choose a different bridge adapter from the available options and confirm the
        transaction in your wallet.

6. Handling BNB Chain RBNT

   1.   Confirm the exact token address in your wallet. Redbelly has not officially verified
        any RBNT contract on BNB Chain.

   2.   Do not attempt to swap it on a BSC decentralized exchange. There is no official
        RBNT liquidity, meaning you risk interacting with copycat pools.

   3.   Because the official Redbelly bridges (Reddex and Lucid) currently only support
        bridging USDC and USDT from BNB Chain, you cannot officially recover BNB
        Chain RBNT to the Redbelly Network at this time.

   4.   Hold the token safely in your wallet until Redbelly officially announces a BNB
        Chain wRBNT contract and an active bridge route.

7. Failure Mode 4: RBNT Sent to the Wrong Exchange Network

Open a support ticket with the exchange immediately. Recovery depends entirely on the
exchange manual-recovery policy. Do not send a second deposit to unlock the first.

To request exchange recovery (MEXC Example):

   1. Open redbelly.routescan.io and paste your wallet address into the search bar.
   2. Click the sending transaction and copy the Transaction Hash.




   3. Scroll to the bottom of the MEXC homepage and click Submit a Ticket under the
      Resources category.

   4. Select the Uncredited Deposit Return Application form.

   5. Fill in the form with the Transaction Hash, deposit address, amount, token (RBNT),
      and the incorrect network you sent on.

   6. Submit the application and wait 1-2 business days for the review.

Success Condition: The exchange manually credits the deposit to your account or
returns the funds to your sending wallet.
